This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 5th April 2026, by
and between Vortexa Analytics Pvt. Ltd., having its registered office at Sector 62, Noida ("Disclosing
Party"), and Meera Consulting Group, having its office at Sector 18, Noida ("Receiving Party"),
collectively the "Parties", in connection with a potential data-analytics partnership (the "Purpose").
1. Background
WHEREAS the Disclosing Party possesses certain proprietary algorithms, datasets, business strategies,
and technical documentation related to its analytics platform; AND WHEREAS the Receiving Party
wishes to evaluate a potential collaboration involving access to certain aspects of this information; NOW
THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows.
2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" shall include, without limitation: (a) all source code, algorithms, and technical
architecture of the Disclosing Party's analytics platform; (b) customer lists, pricing models, and business
strategies; (c) financial statements and projections shared for due diligence purposes; (d) any
information marked "Confidential" or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential
given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure; and (e) the existence, terms, and
status of discussions between the Parties regarding the Purpose.
3. Obligations of Receiving Party
The Receiving Party shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for evaluating the Purpose; (b)
restrict access to Confidential Information to employees and advisors with a genuine need to know, who
are themselves bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those herein; (c) not
reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive the source code or underlying methodology of any
software or algorithms disclosed; (d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing of any unauthorized
use or disclosure of Confidential Information upon becoming aware of the same; and (e) not use the
Confidential Information to develop competing products or services for a period of 2 years from the date
of disclosure.
4. Exclusions from Confidential Information
This Agreement imposes no obligation with respect to Confidential Information that: (a) was already
known to the Receiving Party without confidentiality restrictions prior to disclosure, as evidenced by
written records; (b) becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (c) is independently
developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is
required to be disclosed pursuant to applicable law, regulation, or valid court order, provided that the
Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permitted) to
allow it to seek a protective order.
5. Term and Survival

This Agreement shall remain in effect for 3 years from the date of execution. The confidentiality
obligations set forth in Clause 3 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement for an additional
period of 5 years, except with respect to trade secrets, which shall remain protected for as long as they
retain trade secret status under applicable law.
6. No Obligation to Proceed
Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as an obligation on either Party to proceed with any
transaction, enter into any further agreement, or continue discussions regarding the Purpose. Either
Party may terminate discussions at any time without liability, subject to the surviving obligations under
this Agreement.
7. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of discussions regarding the Purpose,
the Receiving Party shall, within 15 business days, return or destroy all documents and materials
containing Confidential Information, and certify such destruction in writing, save for one archival copy
retained solely for legal compliance purposes.
8. Remedies
The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm not adequately
compensable by monetary damages alone, entitling the non-breaching Party to seek injunctive relief and
specific performance, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the
requirement to post bond.
9. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this
Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved amicably within 30 days; failing which, the dispute
shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Noida,
conducted by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the Parties, with proceedings conducted in English.
10. Miscellaneous
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding its subject matter.
No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. If any provision is held
invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. Neither Party
may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except in connection with
a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

This Service Agreement is made on 15th January 2026, by and between Bluepeak Technologies Pvt.
Ltd., having its office at HSR Layout, Bengaluru ("Client"), and Nirmal Consulting Services, having its
office at Koramangala, Bengaluru ("Service Provider").
1. Scope of Services
The Service Provider shall provide website development and maintenance services to the Client as
detailed in Annexure A.
2. Term
This Agreement shall commence on 20th January 2026 and continue for 6 months, ending on 20th July
2026.
3. Payment Terms
The Client shall pay the Service Provider a total fee of INR 3,00,000, payable in three equal instalments
of INR 1,00,000 each, at the start, midpoint, and completion of the project. Payments shall be made
within 15 days of invoice date.
4. Confidentiality
Both Parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary business information shared during the
engagement and shall not disclose such information to third parties without prior written consent, both
during and for 2 years after termination of this Agreement.
5. Termination
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days written notice to the other Party. In case
of termination, the Client shall pay for all work completed up to the date of termination on a pro-rata
basis.
6. Intellectual Property
Upon full and final payment, all intellectual property rights in the deliverables shall transfer to the Client.
The Service Provider retains the right to showcase the work in its portfolio unless otherwise agreed.
7. Limitation of Liability
Neither Party's liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement,
except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct.
8. Dispute Resolution
Any disputes shall first be resolved through mutual discussion. If unresolved within 30 days, the matter
shall be referred to arbitration in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
9. Governing Law

[PAGE 2]
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, with courts at Bengaluru having jurisdiction.

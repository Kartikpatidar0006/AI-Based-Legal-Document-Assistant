on security deposit refund conditions or deduction terms, no inspection rights clause, no dispute

This Rental Agreement is made on 10th March 2026, by and between Rajesh Kulkarni, residing at
Kothrud, Pune ("Landlord"), and Ankit Sharma, residing at Kothrud, Pune ("Tenant").
1. Property Description
The Landlord agrees to rent out the 2BHK flat located at Flat No. 402, Green Meadows Society, Kothrud,
Pune, to the Tenant.
2. Term of Tenancy
This Agreement shall be valid for 11 months, commencing from 15th March 2026.
3. Rent
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 18,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of each
month.
4. Security Deposit
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of INR 1,00,000 at the time of signing this Agreement.
5. Use of Premises
The Tenant shall use the Premises solely for residential purposes.
6. Maintenance
The Tenant shall be responsible for electricity and water charges.
